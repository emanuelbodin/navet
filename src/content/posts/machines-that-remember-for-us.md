we used to forget on purpose. that was a feature.

now the timeline never blinks. search completes your half-thought. the calendar resurrects a meeting you meant to abandon. convenient — until your past becomes an API with better uptime than your willpower.

## the soft archive

every photo rolled up into the cloud is a little treaty: *you keep this, i stop carrying it*. fine deal, until the keeper starts ranking, suggesting, auto-surfacing. memory with a product roadmap.

```
>>> recall("tuesday")
did you mean: the version that keeps you scrolling?
```

## society, mirrored

institutions always kept records. the difference is latency and intimacy. a clerk in 1890 could not autocomplete your shame. a model in 2026 can approximate it from three likes and a timezone.

so here is the hacky thesis: **tools that remember for us also decide what forgetting costs**. if you cannot forget, you cannot revise. if you cannot revise, you just accumulate.

exit code: think about it. then clear a cache on purpose.
